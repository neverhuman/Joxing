# H12 — The virtual sport is defined well enough for competitive learning

Status: partial implementation; competitive promotion blocked.
Origin: retrospective design summary. Updated:2026-10-29.

- Contract: legal front/side head2points, torso1, guard/incidental/foul surfaces0.
  Direct-zone impulse qualifies one encounter; it does not multiply points.
  Onset motion/face eligibility latches; held contact cannot score repeatedly;
  bell/rearm clocks and stable event IDs prevent reward duplication.
- Delivered: pure rules/referee tests, rigid physical adapter controls, shared
  causal observation/history/timing/replay foundation and saved diagnostics.
- Missing: reviewed surface fitting/self-contact, stable guard/reach, native
  legal-hit/replay proof, fall/foul/exit attribution and known snapshot-alias
  correction. An illegal push must not become a sporting win by shortcut.
- No adopted physiology: simulated forces are uncalibrated; no injury, concussion,
  liver shock or empirical knockout interpretation. Supplied plans are proposals.
- Scale gate:1k→10k→100kvalid completed native games only after motor/contact/
  rules/replay gates and actual held-out learning. Sample diagnostic tapes and
  asynchronous video; retain every episode summary/invalid/not_run count.
