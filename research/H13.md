Status: planned research lead; no implementation or outcome. Origin: prospective.
Updated:2026-10-29.

- Idea: assess QFlex's value-guided exploration after a reproducible baseline
  motor task exists. The authors demonstrate full-body musculoskeletal tasks;
  their results are not Joxing measurements or compatible checkpoint proof.
- Sources: [official project](https://lnsgroup.cc/research/Qflex/) and
  [official code](https://github.com/LNSGroup/Qflex), observed main

- Test/gate: first inspect license, action/decoder/log-probability, observation,

  simulation/optimizer/wall budgets and independent seeds versus ordinary SAC
  on the same qualified native arm/balance task. Use held-out physical survival,
  reach-return, fall/fault rates and total throughput; keep all failed attempts.
- Dependencies: H10 physical interface and baseline replay/source gates. New
  JAX/benchmark environments, any 224-environment example or upstream performance
  claim are not authorization to install, scale hosts or change defaults.
- Outcome: not_run; zero new steps/updates. Decision: defer experimental adoption
  until the measured motor bottleneck and baseline warrant a method comparison.
- Next: complete H10; inspect upstream method/code offline before choosing a
  bounded separate protocol. Do not call this a pretrained-running solution.
