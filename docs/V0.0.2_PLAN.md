# v0.0.2 — muscle-to-contact proof

## Objective and boundary

Demonstrate that a learned MS-Human-700 muscle pulse policy can produce a real glove/target impact, receive one qualified point, withdraw and rearm, and reproduce its event ledger from a mid-contact engine/application snapshot. This is the next integration proof after reaching an invisible point. It is a named **assisted motor diagnostic**, not competitive boxing.

Retain the preceding reach experiment's fixed pelvis and zero gravity to isolate the new contact/referee work. Keep all 700 native muscles and their force/routing properties. Add one explicit 0.283 kg glove and one fixed spherical torso target. Native dynamics integrate every action; no qpos/qvel writes after reset. No force-proportional point reward or rule-threshold relaxation is allowed.

Non-goals: unassisted standing, full external human surface fit, opponent tactics, self-play, official judging, damage/fatigue, GPU parity repair. The full P1–P5 gates in IMPLEMENTATION.md remain necessary. This slice deliberately completes a small dependency between P1 contact evidence and P3 motor learning before tackling full-body competition.

Assumptions/dependencies: pinned anatomy/msgym, locked CPU MuJoCo 3.8.0, existing Rules/HitTracker, local Python and xbabe1 SSH. Current xbabe1 inspection: load ~13 on 128 logical cores, ~38 GiB RAM available, ~331 GB disk free, GPU idle. Cap execution at four CPU workers and one BLAS/OpenMP thread per worker.

## Proof protocol and completion criteria

Frozen proof budget after pilot: seeds 7, 19, 41; **12 generations × 24 candidate episodes per seed**, four workers, fixed final fitted-mean evaluation. Original proposal was 24 × 32; the development pilot reached 8/8 hit-and-withdrawal success using 8 × 16 in 34.8 s, so the proof budget was reduced before evaluating any final scenarios. Thresholds, rules, target variation and physics were not relaxed. Pilot source was 0eba6fe; proof reset IDs and replay scenario were moved to 20000+ to keep final evaluation disjoint from the pilot.

Success is independently measured: exactly one legal torso score, final geometric clearance at least 2 cm, at least 3 cm measured retraction, and referee ready for a fresh encounter. Report hit success and strike-plus-withdrawal success separately. A late contact cannot borrow velocity or impulse from an earlier guard impact. Held pressure never adds another point without real clearance/retraction.

Candidate acceptance: each seed has at least 80% qualified-hit success and at least 75% strike-plus-withdrawal success over its 64 held-out resets; mean paired task-success improvement over initial policy is positive; all attempted evaluations are counted; zero invalid physical rollouts; successful mid-contact ledger replay with final-state max absolute error ≤1e-9. These are diagnostic thresholds, not boxing realism or empirical impact calibration. Failure is a useful iteration result and must remain labeled failed rather than retuning the evaluation or claiming success from a selected clip.

Stop on warnings, unexpected engine resets, nonfinite state/action/force, missing contact staging identities, unregistered target contact, duplicate delivery changing score, physical replay failure, disk/resource cap or systematic action-correlated invalidity. Keep failed attempt IDs and last valid state; no continuation into competitive training.
