## Objective, non-goals and completion

Determine whether motor reward/entropy balance explains the failed v0.0.4 learning result, while making a fixed transition budget practical. Establish whether any improvement survives fresh training seeds and unseen reset scenarios. A negative experiment is a legitimate conclusion; no motor threshold is lowered to obtain a release.

Non-goals: a playable fight, self-play, complete anatomy collision fitting, physiological force calibration, pruning, root surgery, new game points, changed fall criteria or a full three-seed twenty-second motor proof. No hidden support, pose writes or gravity compensation during evaluation. Model/action/observation contracts remain the v0.0.4 native-root/700-muscle/138-group/1,023-value reference.

Dependencies/assumptions: pinned submodules and MuJoCo 3.8.0; locked CPU Torch/SB3 motor extra; fresh shared-host capacity; mechanically accepted reset banks. The previous entropy diagnostic is one state, not causal evidence. SAC optimizes reward plus an entropy term; changing reward scale and changing temperature are distinct interventions. Reference: [SB3 implementation](https://stable-baselines3.readthedocs.io/en/master/_modules/stable_baselines3/sac/sac.html), [original SAC](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.01290), [entropy-reward ablations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.12434). Our installed source and measured receipts take precedence over documentation-version assumptions.

Completion: tested executable wave runner, committed preregistration, every planned experiment assigned completed/failed/incomplete/not_run, all acquired raw evaluation rows and descriptions in Git, independent accounting/replay audit, inspected plot/video, results/conclusions and public GitHub push/tag. Passing the old five-second continuation gate is separate from delivering this experiment wave.

Stop on invalid resets, engine warnings/nonfinite state, unexpected assistance, hash incompatibility, same-host replay failure, missing attempts, resource caps or protocol mutation. Preserve fault/last-valid state; invalidate affected results, halt dependent work and mark unattempted experiments not_run. A physical fall is a valid measured failure. Do not retry silently or change hyperparameters within a registered run.
