## 1. Executive Mission & Baseline Audit (`v0.0.6` $\to$ `v0.0.7`)

### 1.1 What We Have Done vs. What `v0.0.7` Must Solve
Prior iterations established the foundational arena geometry, basic boxer kinematics, and initial strike collision primitives. However, naive RL boxing environments fail due to four classic pathologies:
1. **Rock-'Em-Sock-'Em Flailing Collapse:** Without physical stamina costs on whiffed punches, rotational chin torque, and guard-break dynamics, policies converge to high-frequency mutual button-mashing at point-blank range.
2. **Boolean Guard Exploitation:** Treating blocking as a binary damage multiplier allows agents to hold a permanent "shield" without accounting for physical glove/forearm occlusion vectors, leaving no geometric distinction between a high earmuff guard, a tight peek-a-boo shell, or a low body guard.
3. **Single-Scalar HP Myopia:** A single hit-point bar cannot distinguish between acute concussive stun (flash knockdowns on the chin while off-balance), cumulative visceral body trauma (liver/solar-plexus shots that erode maximum aerobic capacity), and localized arm fatigue.
4. **CPU-Python IPC Bottlenecks:** Stepping individual environments at $60\text{ Hz}$ across process boundaries starves the learner at $<5,000$ Steps Per Second (SPS), whereas true emergent footwork, slipping, parrying, and counter-punching require $10^8$–$10^9$ environment frames ($>200,000$ SPS on CPU SIMD; $>1,000,000$ SPS on GPU).

### 1.2 Core `v0.0.7` Deliverables
* **Canonical Dual-Spawn Arena:** Red Boxer ($B_0$) and Blue Boxer ($B_1$) spawn deterministically at mirrored starting coordinates at the start of every bout and post-knockdown reset, inside a $6.10\text{ m} \times 6.10\text{ m}$ elastic-rope ring.
* **Hybrid SLIP-Base + 3D Articulated Upper-Body Physics:** Lower-body Zero-Moment-Point (ZMP) / Spring-Loaded Inverted Pendulum (SLIP) footwork base coupled to a 14-capsule articulated upper body (pelvis, abdomen, thorax, neck/cranium, shoulders, upper arms, forearms, gloves) solved at $f_{\text{phys}} = 240\text{ Hz}$ ($4$ substeps per $f_{\text{RL}} = 60\text{ Hz}$ policy decision).
* **5-Pool Physiological & Biomechanical Damage Engine:** Separate state differential equations for Acute Cranial Stun ($S_{\text{stun}}$), Cumulative Cranial Trauma ($D_{\text{head}}$), Visceral/Thoracic Body Damage & Liver Shock ($D_{\text{body}}, S_{\text{liver}}$), Dual-Arm Guard Integrity ($G_L, G_R$), and Two-Compartment Stamina ($E_{\text{fast}}, E_{\max}$).
* **3D Swept-Capsule Active Defense System:** Continuous Collision Detection (CCD) resolving glove-on-forearm, glove-on-glove, and glove-on-shoulder intersections with three mechanical regimes: **Braced Shell Absorption**, **Timed Vector Parry/Deflection**, and **Slip/Weave Near-Miss Evasion**.
* **Professional 10-Point Must + Quantitative CompuBox Judge Ledger:** Decouples official bout judging (clean effective punching, defense/evasion, counter-punching, ring generalship) from the dense, zero-sum/general-sum RL shaping reward.
* **Prioritized Fictitious Self-Play (PFSP) League + HPC Vectorized Engine:** Ego-centric symmetry-canonicalized observation space ($\mathbb{R}^{108}$), continuous/hybrid PD action space ($\mathbb{R}^{18}$), Asymmetric Recurrent PPO, and SoA SIMD / `wgpu` mega-kernel execution.

## 3. Biomechanical Damage Tracking & 5-Pool Physiological Engine

Health is **never** represented as a single scalar. Each boxer maintains a deterministic physiological state struct `PhysiologyState` updated at $240\text{ Hz}$.

### 3.1 Effective Impact Impulse Calculation
When Boxer $A$'s glove (`13` or `14`) collides with Boxer $B$'s target surface at contact point $\mathbf{p}_c$ with contact normal $\mathbf{n}_c$:
1. **Relative Closure Velocity:** $\mathbf{v}_{\text{rel}} = \mathbf{v}_{\text{glove},A}(\mathbf{p}_c) - \mathbf{v}_{\text{target},B}(\mathbf{p}_c)$. Only approaching contacts ($v_n = -\mathbf{v}_{\text{rel}} \cdot \mathbf{n}_c > v_{\min} = 1.2\text{ m/s}$) register as strikes (pushing/clinching below $1.2\text{ m/s}$ generates zero strike damage).
2. **Effective Kinetic Mass ($m_{\text{eff}}$):** A boxer who plants their feet ($\mathcal{B}_{t,A} > 0.8$) and rotates the trunk ($\omega_{\text{trunk},A}$ aligned with punch direction) couples torso mass into the glove:
   $$m_{\text{eff}} = m_{\text{arm}} + \kappa_{\text{chain}} \cdot \mathcal{B}_{t,A} \cdot \sigma(\hat{\mathbf{v}}_{\text{glove}} \cdot (\boldsymbol{\omega}_{\text{trunk},A} \times \mathbf{r}_{\text{shoulder}})) \cdot m_{\text{torso}}$$
   where $m_{\text{arm}} = 4.2\text{ kg}$, $m_{\text{torso}} = 38.0\text{ kg}$, and $\kappa_{\text{chain}} = 0.22$. Arm-punches thrown while retreating have low $m_{\text{eff}}$; planted hooks/crosses carry high $m_{\text{eff}}$.
3. **Raw Normal & Tangential Impulse:**
   $$J_n = (1 + e_{\text{glove}}) \frac{m_{\text{eff}} m_{\text{target}}}{m_{\text{eff}} + m_{\text{target}}} v_n, \qquad \mathbf{J}_t = -\min(\mu_f J_n, m_{\text{eff}} \|\mathbf{v}_{\text{rel},t}\|) \hat{\mathbf{v}}_{\text{rel},t}$$
   Strikes below the noise floor $J_n < J_{\text{min}} = 3.5\text{ N}\cdot\text{s}$ are discarded as light touches.

### 3.2 Pool 1: Cranial Concussive Trauma & Rotational Stun ($S_{\text{stun}}, D_{\text{head}}$)
Cranial trauma depends on both linear impulse $J_n$ and **cervical rotational torque** around the neck pivot $\mathbf{p}_{\text{neck}}$:
$$\mathcal{T}_{\text{cranial}} = J_n \cdot \mu_{\text{zone}} \cdot \left(1.0 + \lambda_{\text{rot}} \frac{\|(\mathbf{p}_c - \mathbf{p}_{\text{neck}}) \times \mathbf{J}\|}{r_{\text{chin}} J_n}\right) \cdot \left(1.0 + 0.5(1.0 - \mathcal{B}_{t,B})\right) \cdot (1.0 - \eta_{\text{guard}})$$
where $\lambda_{\text{rot}} = 0.85$ rewards lateral hooks to the jaw/temple that twist the head, and $\eta_{\text{guard}} \in [0, 0.92]$ is the active guard attenuation factor (Section 4).

* **Cumulative Head Damage ($D_{\text{head}} \in [0, 100]$):**
  $$D_{\text{head}}(t + \Delta t) = \min\left(100.0,\; D_{\text{head}}(t) + c_{\text{head\_cum}} \mathcal{T}_{\text{cranial}}\right)$$
* **Acute Stun / Concussion Gauge ($S_{\text{stun}} \in [0, 100]$):**
  $$S_{\text{stun}}(t + \Delta t) = \max\left(0.0,\; S_{\text{stun}}(t) - k_{\text{recov}}(D_{\text{head}}, E_{\text{fast}}) \Delta t + c_{\text{stun}} \mathcal{T}_{\text{cranial}}\right)$$
  where recovery rate degrades as cumulative head trauma increases ("chin erosion"):
  $$k_{\text{recov}}(D_{\text{head}}, E_{\text{fast}}) = k_{\text{recov},0} \cdot \left(1.0 - 0.65 \frac{D_{\text{head}}}{100.0}\right) \cdot \left(0.4 + 0.6 \frac{E_{\text{fast}}}{E_{\max}}\right)$$
* **Knockdown Trigger Condition:**
  A cranial knockdown is triggered immediately if:
  1. **Concussive Overload:** $S_{\text{stun}} \ge 100.0$, OR
  2. **Flash Off-Balance Knockdown:** $\mathcal{T}_{\text{cranial}} \ge \mathcal{T}_{\text{flash}}$ while $\mathcal{B}_{t,B} < 0.38$ and $S_{\text{stun}} \ge 65.0$.

### 3.3 Pool 2: Visceral/Thoracic Body Trauma & Liver Shock ($D_{\text{body}}, S_{\text{liver}}$)
Body punches invest in long-term attrition and acute visceral paralysis:
$$\mathcal{T}_{\text{body}} = J_n \cdot \mu_{\text{zone}} \cdot \left(1.0 + 0.40 \cdot \mathbb{I}[\text{target\_committing\_punch}]\right) \cdot (1.0 - \eta_{\text{guard}})$$
*(Note: Catching an opponent on the liver or solar plexus while they are mid-punch adds a $+40\%$ diaphragm-relaxed multiplier).*

* **Cumulative Body Trauma ($D_{\text{body}} \in [0, 100]$) & Maximum Stamina Ceiling Decay:**
  $$D_{\text{body}}(t + \Delta t) = \min\left(100.0,\; D_{\text{body}}(t) + c_{\text{body\_cum}} \mathcal{T}_{\text{body}}\right)$$
  $$E_{\max}(t) = E_{\max,0} \cdot \max\left(0.35,\; \exp\left(-0.011 \cdot D_{\text{body}}(t)\right)\right)$$
* **Acute Visceral / Liver Shock ($S_{\text{liver}} \in [0, 100]$):**
  Hits to the `Liver` (`2`) or `Solar Plexus` (`3`) spheres increment $S_{\text{liver}}$, which decays at $8.0\text{ units/s}$.
  * While $S_{\text{liver}} > 50.0$: Stamina regeneration is frozen ($0\text{ units/s}$) and footwork surge/strafe speed is reduced by $35\%$.
  * If $S_{\text{liver}} \ge 100.0$: Triggers a **Delayed Body-Shot Knockdown** (fighter drops to one knee after a $0.35\text{ s}$ autonomic lag).

### 3.4 Pool 3: Dual-Arm Guard Structural Integrity ($G_L, G_R \in [0, 100]$)
Each arm has its own guard integrity pool initialized to $100.0$.
* When a forearm/glove absorbs an incoming impulse $J_n$, the defending arm's integrity drops by:
  $$\Delta G_{\text{arm}} = -c_{\text{guard\_wear}} J_n \cdot \left(1.25 - 0.50 \cdot u_{\text{guard}}\right) + k_{\text{guard\_regen}} \cdot \mathbb{I}[\text{no\_block\_for } 1.0\text{ s}] \Delta t$$
* **Guard Break Mechanics:** When $G_{\text{arm}} < 30.0$, the arm's maximum PD joint stiffness $k_p$ scales down linearly to $40\%$ of nominal, and guard bleed-through increases from $\xi_{\text{bleed}} = 0.08$ up to $0.45$. If $G_{\text{arm}} = 0.0$, a heavy strike triggers a **Guard-Break Stagger** ($250\text{ ms}$ forced guard opening).

### 3.5 Pool 4: Two-Compartment Bioenergetic Stamina ($E_{\text{fast}} \in [0, E_{\max}(t)]$)
* **Stamina Drain Differential Equation:**
  $$\frac{dE_{\text{fast}}}{dt} = -P_{\text{strike}} - P_{\text{whiff}} - P_{\text{loco}} - P_{\text{brace}} + R_{\text{aerobic}}$$
  * **Strike Commitment Cost ($P_{\text{strike}}$):** Proportional to integrated squared actuator torque $\sum_{\text{arm}} \tau_i^2$.
  * **Eccentric Whiff Penalty ($P_{\text{whiff}}$):** If a committed punch ($v_{\text{glove}} > 4.5\text{ m/s}$) reaches full extension without making any contact (clean hit, block, or parry), the boxer pays an immediate **$1.45\times$ eccentric deceleration stamina surcharge** and a $140\text{ ms}$ recovery lag. This single biomechanically grounded rule eliminates RL punch-spamming.
  * **Brace Cost ($P_{\text{brace}}$):** Holding maximum guard brace tension ($u_{\text{guard}} > 0.8$) reduces passive stamina regeneration by $60\%$ (preventing permanent braced turtling).
* **Gassed-Out Regime ($E_{\text{fast}} < 25.0$):** Peak shoulder/elbow actuator torque scales by $\sqrt{\max(0.20, E_{\text{fast}} / 25.0)}$, slowing hand speed and guard recovery.

### 3.6 Knockdown Recovery & TKO / KO State Machine
When a knockdown occurs:
1. Both fighters freeze combat; the standing fighter is routed to the furthest neutral corner.
2. The downed fighter enters an 8-to-10 second recovery mini-process governed by cumulative trauma:
   $$T_{\text{rise}} = 3.0 + 0.045 D_{\text{head}} + 0.025 D_{\text{body}} + 1.8 \cdot N_{\text{KD\_total}} - 2.2 \left(\frac{E_{\text{fast}}}{E_{\max,0}}\right)$$
3. **KO:** If $T_{\text{rise}} \ge 9.95\text{ s}$, the fight ends immediately by **Knockout (KO)**.
4. **3-Knockdown TKO Rule:** Three knockdowns in the same round (or 4 across the bout) immediately end the fight by **Technical Knockout (TKO)**.
5. **Post-Knockdown Continuation:** If $T_{\text{rise}} < 9.95\text{ s}$ (mandatory 8-count minimum), the downed boxer rises with $S_{\text{stun}}$ reset to $35.0 + 0.3 D_{\text{head}}$, and both fighters resume from their current ring positions (or canonical spawn if between rounds).

## 4. How Blocking, Parrying & Evasion Work (3D Swept-Capsule Mechanics)

Blocking in Joxing is **purely geometric and mechanical**—there is no global "block state" that magically protects uncovered hitboxes.

### 4.1 Continuous Collision Detection (CCD) Occlusion Ordering
During each $240\text{ Hz}$ physics substep ($\Delta t = 4.167\text{ ms}$), each attacking glove (`13`, `14`) sweeps a capsule from $\mathbf{x}_{\text{glove}}(t)$ to $\mathbf{x}_{\text{glove}}(t + \Delta t)$.
We compute the earliest time-of-impact $t_{\text{TOI}} \in [0, 1]$ against all candidate colliders on the defender:
$$\mathcal{C}_{\text{def}} = \{\text{Glove}_{L/R},\; \text{Forearm}_{L/R},\; \text{Shoulder}_{L/R},\; \text{Cranium},\; \text{Mandible},\; \text{UpperTorso},\; \text{LowerTorso},\; \text{Liver},\; \text{SolarPlexus}\}$$

### 4.3 Why No Single Guard Position Is Invulnerable (Tactical Geometry)
Because forearms are finite capsules ($r = 0.055\text{ m}$, $L = 0.26\text{ m}$) and gloves are finite spheres ($r = 0.085\text{ m}$):
* **High Front Guard (Peek-a-Boo):** Forearms vertical and parallel in front of nose/chin. Blocks straight jabs (`lead`) and crosses (`rear`) completely, but leaves lateral temples (`5`) open to wide hooks and lower ribs/liver (`1, 2`) exposed to body hooks.
* **High Wide Guard (Earmuff):** Forearms framing the sides of the cranium. Blocks left/right hooks to the jaw/temple, but leaves a $0.14\text{ m}$ central vertical seam vulnerable to straight split-jabs and uppercuts, plus an open midsection.
* **Cross-Arm / Philly Shell Guard:** Lead forearm draped horizontally across the abdomen/liver (`1, 2, 3`), lead shoulder (`7`) elevated to shield the left chin (`6`), rear glove (`14`) braced against the right jaw. Protects the body and deflects straight crosses via shoulder roll, but requires active trunk yaw and leaves the high lead-side temple vulnerable to overhand rights and lead hooks if mistimed.

## 5. Sophisticated Point System & Dual-Layer Scoring Architecture

Joxing separates the **Official Championship Judging System** (which decides rounds, decisions, and fighter ratings) from the **Dense RL Shaping Reward** (which guides policy gradients every $16.67\text{ ms}$).

### 5.1 Layer A: Quantitative CompuBox + 10-Point Must Judge Ledger
During each round $r \in \{1, \dots, N_{\text{rounds}}\}$ ($T_{\text{round}} = 60.0\text{ s}$ simulated combat time, configurable up to $180.0\text{ s}$), the referee engine accumulates four sub-scores for each boxer $i \in \{0, 1\}$:

1. **Clean Effective Punching Score ($S_{\text{clean},i}$):**
   Every strike $k$ landing on the opponent's head or body with $\eta_{\text{guard}} < 0.35$ contributes:
   $$s_{\text{hit},k} = \left(\frac{J_{n,k}}{J_{\text{ref}}}\right)^{1.15} \cdot \mu_{\text{zone},k} \cdot (1.0 - \eta_{\text{guard},k}) \cdot M_{\text{counter},k} \cdot M_{\text{combo},k}$$
   * **Counter-Punch Multiplier ($M_{\text{counter},k}$):** Equals $1.35$ if the opponent was in an active punch commitment or whiff-recovery state within the last $180\text{ ms}$; otherwise $1.0$.
   * **Combination Multiplier ($M_{\text{combo},k}$):** If landed within $0.80\text{ s}$ of a previous clean hit by the *other* hand or to a *different* target level (Head $\leftrightarrow$ Body), $M_{\text{combo},k} = 1.0 + 0.12 \min(n_{\text{chain}} - 1, 3)$. Consecutive identical single-hand spam receives $M_{\text{combo},k} = 0.85$.

2. **Trauma & Significant Strike Impact Score ($S_{\text{impact},i}$):**
   Rewards visibly staggering or hurting the opponent:
   $$S_{\text{impact},i} = 0.45 \Delta D_{\text{head},\text{opp}} + 0.35 \Delta D_{\text{body},\text{opp}} + 4.0 \cdot N_{\text{stagger},\text{opp}}$$
   where a stagger event triggers whenever a single strike spikes $S_{\text{stun},\text{opp}}$ by $\ge 28.0$ or $S_{\text{liver},\text{opp}}$ by $\ge 35.0$.

3. **Defensive Craft & Neutralization Score ($S_{\text{def},i}$):**
   $$S_{\text{def},i} = \sum_{m \in \text{Evaded}} 1.80 \cdot \left(\frac{v_{\text{opp\_glove},m}}{v_{\text{ref}}}\right) + \sum_{p \in \text{Parried}} 2.10 + \sum_{b \in \text{BracedBlock}} 0.35 \cdot \eta_{\text{guard},b}$$
   *(Note: Slipping/weaving and active parrying score $>5\times$ higher than passive shell blocking, incentivizing slick defensive mastery).*

4. **Ring Generalship & Effective Aggression ($S_{\text{ring},i}$):**
    Evaluated at $10\text{ Hz}$ only when the distance between fighters is within tactical engagement range ($d_{AB} \in [0.65\text{ m}, 2.10\text{ m}]$):
   $$S_{\text{ring},i} = \frac{1}{T_{\text{round}}} \int_0^{T_{\text{round}}} \mathbb{I}[d_{AB}(t) \le 2.1\text{ m}] \left(0.60 \frac{r_{\text{rope},\text{opp}}(t) - r_{\text{rope},i}(t)}{3.05} + 0.40 (\hat{\mathbf{v}}_i \cdot \hat{\mathbf{d}}_{i \to \text{opp}}) \cdot \mathbb{I}[\text{landing\_rate}_i > 0.25]\right) dt$$
   *(Coming forward while missing everything yields zero Effective Aggression; cutting off the ring and backing the opponent toward the ropes while landing $\ge 25\%$ of thrown strikes scores positive Ring Generalship).*

#### Round Dominance Index & 10-Point Must Mapping
At the end of round $r$, compute the Round Dominance Differential $\Delta \mathcal{D}_r = \mathcal{D}_{r,0} - \mathcal{D}_{r,1}$ where:
$$\mathcal{D}_{r,i} = 0.45 S_{\text{clean},i} + 0.25 S_{\text{impact},i} + 0.18 S_{\text{def},i} + 0.12 S_{\text{ring},i}$$
Let $\text{RelDiff}_r = \frac{\Delta \mathcal{D}_r}{\max(10.0, \mathcal{D}_{r,0} + \mathcal{D}_{r,1})}$. Before knockdown deductions:
* $\vert{}\text{RelDiff}_r\vert{} < 0.04 \implies 10\text{–}10$ (Even Round)
* $0.04 \le \vert{}\text{RelDiff}_r\vert{} < 0.38 \implies 10\text{–}9$ for the round winner
* $\vert{}\text{RelDiff}_r\vert{} \ge 0.38 \text{ and } S_{\text{impact},\text{winner}} \ge 15.0 \implies 10\text{–}8$ (Dominant Round without KD)
* **Knockdown Deductions:** Each knockdown suffered by boxer $i$ in round $r$ subtracts $1$ point from boxer $i$'s score for that round (capped at $10\text{–}6$).

### 6.2 Continuous PD + Biomechanical Action Space ($\mathcal{A} \in [-1, 1]^{18}$)
Sampled at $f_{\text{RL}} = 60\text{ Hz}$ and held/interpolated across $4$ physics substeps ($240\text{ Hz}$):
* `a[0..3]` **Footwork Velocity & Yaw:** Target surge speed $v_x \in [-2.4, +3.0]\text{ m/s}$, strafe speed $v_y \in [-2.5, +2.5]\text{ m/s}$, yaw rate $\omega_z \in [-4.5, +4.5]\text{ rad/s}$.
* `a[3..5]` **Stance Width & Pivot Dash Impulse:** `a[3]` modulates target stance width $w_{\text{stance}} \in [0.32, 0.65]\text{ m}$; `a[4] > 0.65` triggers a short $120\text{ ms}$ slip-step/pivot burst (cooldown $400\text{ ms}$, cost $4.5$ stamina).
* `a[5..8]` **Trunk Weave / Slip / Roll PD Targets:** Target waist pitch $\theta_{\text{pitch}} \in [-0.45, +0.35]\text{ rad}$, waist roll (lateral head slip) $\theta_{\text{roll}} \in [-0.50, +0.50]\text{ rad}$, waist axial rotation (punch torque) $\theta_{\text{yaw}} \in [-0.65, +0.65]\text{ rad}$.
* `a[8..13]` **Left Arm Target & Impedance:**
  * `a[8..11]`: Target 3D wrist position in shoulder-local spherical/Cartesian workspace (covers both close guard anchors and full $0.78\text{ m}$ punch extension).
  * `a[11]`: **Elbow Roll / Hook Plane Angle** $\phi_{\text{elbow},L} \in [0, \pi/2]$ ($0 =$ vertical forearm for straight jab/uppercut/high-guard; $\pi/2 =$ horizontal forearm for hook/overhand/cross-guard).
  * `a[12]`: **Strike Snap vs. Guard Brace Control ($u_L \in [-1, 1]$):**
    * When $u_L < 0$ and forearm is near the head/torso: activates **Guard Brace Tension** $u_{\text{guard},L} = -u_L \in (0, 1]$.
    * When $u_L > 0$: scales shoulder/elbow fast-twitch punch drive gain $k_{p,\text{strike}} = k_{p,0}(1 + 1.8 u_L)$.
* `a[13..18]` **Right Arm Target & Impedance:** Symmetrical to `a[8..13]` for the right arm.

### 6.3 Dense RL Reward Shaping Function ($R_{t,i}$)
To prevent zero-sum stalling while ensuring convergence to championship-winning policies, the instantaneous reward at step $t$ combines a **Zero-Sum Combat Differential**, an **Individual Biomechanical Discipline Term**, and a **Sparse Round/Bout Terminal Outcome**:

$$R_{t,i} = \lambda_{\text{dense}}(k) \cdot \left( r_{\text{combat},i} - \gamma_{\text{zs}} r_{\text{combat},\text{opp}} + r_{\text{discipline},i} \right) + r_{\text{terminal},i}$$

1. **Combat Differential Term ($r_{\text{combat},i}$, with $\gamma_{\text{zs}} = 0.85$):**
   $$r_{\text{combat},i} = w_{\text{hit}} \Delta S_{\text{clean},i} + w_{\text{trauma}} (1.5 \Delta D_{\text{head},\text{opp}} + 1.1 \Delta D_{\text{body},\text{opp}} + 0.4 \Delta S_{\text{stun},\text{opp}}) + w_{\text{def}} \Delta S_{\text{def},i} + w_{\text{ring}} \Delta S_{\text{ring},i} + 15.0 \cdot \mathbb{I}[\text{KD}_{\text{scored}}]$$
2. **Biomechanical & Tactical Discipline Term ($r_{\text{discipline},i}$):**
   $$r_{\text{discipline},i} = -0.015 \|a_t - a_{t-1}\|_2^2 - 0.008 \sum \tau_j^2 - 0.35 \cdot \mathbb{I}[\text{whiff\_event}] - 0.04 \cdot \mathbb{I}[\text{hands\_dropped\_in\_range}] + 0.02 \cdot \mathcal{B}_{t,i}$$
   * *Hands-Dropped-In-Range Penalty:* Applies only when $d_{AB} < 1.35\text{ m}$ (inside striking pocket), neither arm is actively punching, and *both* gloves are below collarbone height without trunk slip velocity. Outside $1.35\text{ m}$, fighters are free to drop hands to rest stamina!
   * *Engagement Pacing (Anti-Stalling):* If $d_{AB} > 2.0\text{ m}$ for more than $3.5\text{ s}$ without a strike attempt, the fighter further from the ring center $(0,0)$ receives $-0.05/\text{step}$.
3. **Sparse Terminal Reward ($r_{\text{terminal},i}$):**
   * Round End: $+5.0 \times (\text{RoundPoints}_i - \text{RoundPoints}_{\text{opp}})$.
   * Bout Win by KO/TKO: $+40.0$ (Winner), $-40.0$ (Loser).
   * Bout Win by Decision: $+20.0$ (Winner), $-20.0$ (Loser).

### 6.4 Asymmetric Recurrent PPO + Prioritized Fictitious Self-Play (PFSP) League
* **Actor-Critic Architecture:**
  * **Actor $\pi_\theta(a_t \mid o_t, h_t)$:** `Linear(108 -> 512) -> LayerNorm -> SiLU -> Linear(512 -> 256) -> LayerNorm -> SiLU -> GRUCell(256, 256) -> Linear(256 -> 36)` (18 means $\mu_a$, 18 log-stds $\log \sigma_a$).
  * **Asymmetric Privileged Critic $V_\phi(s_t, h_t)$:** Receives $o_t$ concatenated with the opponent's exact privileged physiological state ($E_{\text{fast},\text{opp}}, E_{\max,\text{opp}}, S_{\text{stun},\text{opp}}, S_{\text{liver},\text{opp}}, G_{L,\text{opp}}, G_{R,\text{opp}}$) ($108 + 14 = 122$ dims) $\to$ `MLP(512 -> 256) -> GRUCell(256, 256) -> Linear(256 -> 1)`.
* **PFSP League Matchmaking Distribution:**
  Every parallel arena $e \in \{0, \dots, N_{\text{env}}-1\}$ samples an opponent policy at episode reset according to:
  * **$50\%$ Latest Self-Play:** Current policy $\pi_\theta$ vs. $\pi_{\theta_{\text{EMA}}}$ ($\tau_{\text{EMA}} = 0.995$).
  * **$30\%$ Prioritized Historical Pool ($\mathcal{M}_{\text{hist}}$):** Checkpoints saved every $5 \times 10^6$ steps. Opponent $M_k \in \mathcal{M}_{\text{hist}}$ is sampled with probability proportional to $p(M_k) \propto \text{WinRate}(\pi_\theta, M_k) \cdot (1 - \text{WinRate}(\pi_\theta, M_k))^{2.0}$, focusing training on historical versions that exploit current blind spots.
  * **$15\%$ Style-Conditioned Archetypes:** Opponents conditioned on distinct $\mathbf{w}_{\text{style}}$ vectors (Swarmer, Out-Boxer, Counter-Puncher, Slugger) with style-specific reward modifiers.
  * **$5\%$ Deterministic Calibration Bots:**
    * `Bot_ShellGuard`: Keeps high guard braced, throws counter 1–2s after blocking.
    * `Bot_JabMover`: Circles ring, throws fast lead jabs at $1.1\text{ m}$ range.
    * `Bot_SwarmerHook`: Steps inside, throws alternating body-head hooks.

## 7. High-Performance Computing (HPC) Speed-Up Architecture

To train sophisticated 3D biomechanical boxing in hours rather than weeks, Joxing implements a **Two-Tier Data-Oriented HPC Engine** in Rust.

### 7.1 Tier 1: Cache-Aligned SoA SIMD CPU Engine (`crates/joxing-sim`)
Standard OOP game loops (`Vec<Boxer>`) fail on CPU due to pointer chasing and branch misprediction. `joxing-sim` enforces **Structure-of-Arrays (SoA)** layout across batches of $B = 64$ arenas per chunk (`#[repr(C, align(64))]`):

```rust
#[repr(C, align(64))]
pub struct ArenaChunkSoA {
    // 64 Arenas * 2 Boxers = 128 Boxers packed contiguously for AVX2 (f32x8) / AVX-512 (f32x16) / NEON (f32x4)
    pub pelvis_x: [f32; 128],
    pub pelvis_y: [f32; 128],
    pub pelvis_z: [f32; 128],
    pub pelvis_yaw: [f32; 128],
    pub balance: [f32; 128],
    
    // 14 Capsules per boxer -> [14][128] contiguous SoA endpoints (ax, ay, az, bx, by, bz, r)
    pub cap_ax: [[f32; 128]; 14],
    pub cap_ay: [[f32; 128]; 14],
    pub cap_az: [[f32; 128]; 14],
    pub cap_bx: [[f32; 128]; 14],
    pub cap_by: [[f32; 128]; 14],
    pub cap_bz: [[f32; 128]; 14],

    // 5-Pool Physiology State
    pub stamina_fast: [f32; 128],
    pub stamina_max: [f32; 128],
    pub stun_acute: [f32; 128],
    pub dmg_head_cum: [f32; 128],
    pub dmg_body_cum: [f32; 128],
    pub shock_liver: [f32; 128],
    pub guard_left: [f32; 128],
    pub guard_right: [f32; 128],
}

```

#### Key Algorithmic Speed-Ups in `joxing-sim`:

1. **Zero Broadphase / Fixed-Pair Branchless Narrowphase CCD:**
* A general physics engine runs an $O(N \log N)$ BVH broadphase and heap-allocated contact manifold solver.
* In Joxing, **only 2 attacking gloves per boxer can generate strike contacts against 9 target capsules on the opponent** ($2 \times 2 \times 9 = 36$ fixed capsule-capsule pairs per arena!).
* We evaluate all $36$ pairs using a **100% branchless SIMD Closest-Point-Between-Segments kernel** (`simd_capsule_ccd_36`). Using `std::simd` / `wide::f32x8`, 8 arenas ($288$ capsule pairs) execute in $<420$ CPU clock cycles with zero heap allocations and zero branch mispredictions.


2. **Unrolled Analytical 2-Link Inverse/Forward Kinematics + PD Solver:**
* Instead of solving a $14 \times 14$ dense Featherstone mass matrix via LU decomposition, the torso-head chain (`3` links) and each 2-link arm (Shoulder $\to$ Elbow $\to$ Wrist) are solved via closed-form trigonometric updates and explicit symplectic Euler with analytical joint clamping at $240\text{ Hz}$.


3. **Lock-Free Rayon Rollout Pool:**
* Worker threads step `ArenaChunkSoA` blocks in parallel via `rayon::par_iter_mut()`, writing observations directly into pre-allocated pinned memory tensors (`[T, N_env, 2, 108]`) shared zero-copy with the tensor backend.



### 7.2 Tier 2: `wgpu` / CUDA Fused Mega-Kernel (`crates/joxing-gpu`)

For maximum throughput on Apple Silicon (Metal), NVIDIA (Vulkan/CUDA), or AMD GPUs, the entire $240\text{ Hz}$ physics substep loop is fused into a **single WGSL Compute Shader (`joxing_step.wgsl`)**:

* **Thread Mapping:** `@workgroup_size(64, 1, 1)` where **1 GPU invocation = 1 complete boxing arena (both Red and Blue boxers)**.
* **Register-Resident Substepping:** Each GPU thread loads its arena's state from storage buffers once, executes **all $4$ physics substeps ($240\text{ Hz}$) inside GPU registers** (including forward kinematics, 36-pair swept-capsule CCD, 5-pool trauma integration, and CompuBox judge accumulators), and writes out the new state and the two $\mathbb{R}^{108}$ ego-centric observations at $60\text{ Hz}$.
* **VRAM-Resident Rollout Loop:** The GPU storage buffer binding for `obs_buffer` is shared directly with the GPU neural network inference pass (`burn-wgpu` / `candle` / `cudarc`), eliminating PCIe host-device copies during rollout collection.

| Execution Backend | Target Batch Size ($N_{\text{env}}$) | Control Rate ($f_{\text{RL}}$) | Physics Rate ($f_{\text{phys}}$) | Required Throughput Gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Scalar Reference (Debug)** | $1$ | $60\text{ Hz}$ | $240\text{ Hz}$ | $\ge 15,000\text{ SPS}$ (1 core) |
| **CPU SIMD + Rayon (`joxing-sim`)** | $512$ ($8$ chunks of $64$) | $60\text{ Hz}$ | $240\text{ Hz}$ | **$\ge 220,000\text{ SPS}$** (16-core CPU) |
| **GPU Mega-Kernel (`joxing-gpu`)** | $8,192$ ($128$ workgroups) | $60\text{ Hz}$ | $240\text{ Hz}$ | **$\ge 1,250,000\text{ SPS}$** (Single GPU) |
