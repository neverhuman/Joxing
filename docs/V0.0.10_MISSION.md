## Objective and decisions

Can a small learned correction to the qualified native teacher extend physical survival beyond its roughly five-second collapse? Preserve all700 muscles, native2ms physics,20ms controls, signed substep evidence and physical failure criteria. The native actor remains immutable. Learn a separate138-dimensional residual with a fully specified deterministic700-muscle expansion; evaluate physical survival independently from the training reward. This avoids blindly resuming the upstream DynSyn stochastic decoder: its action_log_prob returns the138 group distribution log probability while the decoder independently samples562 weights, and clamps the result. That code is a research implementation, not a verified700-dimensional action density.

A deterministic environment transform of a138-dimensional SAC action has an explicit latent-action objective. Its entropy is over those138 residual actions, never claimed to be the density of700 excitations. Use ordinary SB3 SAC for the new residual; do not modify third_party or reuse the incompatible1,023-feature stance critic/replay. Freeze teacher features, actor, decoder amplitude0.075 and normalizer. A zero residual must reproduce the current teacher exactly. This is a motor policy variant, not a new anatomy/scoring rule.

Non-goals: competitive self-play, a stationary boxing guard, new equipment/physics/reference repairs, GPU physics, latent decoder retraining, many hyperparameter conditions, a long unchanged training sweep,100,000-game claims or changing v8 failure thresholds. The CPU scaling evidence supports four persistent native workers for this pilot; it does not predict optimizer or boxing throughput. No GPU/driver/service installation or purchases. The global16-worker ceiling remains; this mission uses at most4.

Dependencies: completed/published v9 evidence, pinned all700 model, exact source actor/normalizer/config hashes from v8/v9, source-v8 saved replay/actions/contact tails, working locked CPU runtime, fresh xbabe3 capacity and exclusive owned-job lease. Preserve all old checkouts and protocols.
